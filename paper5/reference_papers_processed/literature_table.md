- **Corpus size:** 79 unique papers.
- **Tier:** `D` = direct nearest neighbor (22); `S` = strongly related method (32); `F` = foundation/adjacent evidence (25).
- **Publication:** a named venue is recorded only when confirmed by a primary proceedings page or the paper's arXiv metadata. Otherwise it is `formal venue UNVERIFIED`.
- **Code:** only a paper-linked or otherwise official repository is recorded; otherwise `UNVERIFIED`.
- **Cost:** qualitative cost is reported when the paper establishes the regime; missing exact GPU-hours/tokens are `UNVERIFIED` rather than inferred.
- **Hardware abbreviations:** `P/D` = prefill/decode; `KV` = key-value cache; `HIL` = hardware in the loop.
